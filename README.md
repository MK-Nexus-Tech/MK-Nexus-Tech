## 👋 About Me

I am a **Solutions Architect and Founder of MK Nexus Tech**, focused on designing and building **Business Intelligence systems, executive dashboards, reporting platforms, and operational applications**.

I help businesses turn fragmented operational data into **structured, decision-ready intelligence**.

Most businesses don't have a data problem.

They have a **visibility problem**.

Data is often scattered across spreadsheets, SaaS platforms, operational tools, and disconnected reporting workflows. This creates slow reporting, inconsistent KPIs, duplicated work, and limited executive visibility.

My work focuses on building systems that provide:

- Centralized business visibility

# 🧠 Architecture Approach

I don't start with charts.

I start with the **business decision the system needs to support**.

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Data Sources
       ↓
Data Architecture
       ↓
Data Model
       ↓
KPI & Business Logic
       ↓
Intelligence Layer
       ↓
User Interface
       ↓
Decision Support
````

A dashboard is only useful when it helps someone make a better decision.

My systems therefore prioritize **business context, KPI logic, usability, reliability, and operational clarity**.
